how i make it

Not every problem is the same so does the process. When I say I designed this, I meant I have researched about the problem and the root cause, ideate bunch of concepts on a paper, built prototype of selected concepts, let the real user try them and iterate on the weak spots until it solves the problem without creating another problem.

When i make it

I don't separate the engineer in me from the designer in me — one shows me how something gets made, the other shows me why it matters once it's out in the world. I believe the people who build a product deserve as much thought as the people who use it. So that's how I design: for the hands that make it, and the hands that hold it.
